Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Ultramain Batch Planner ("the App") is a personal content planning tool used by a single TikTok creator (@ultramain) to plan a weekly batch of posts, generate caption and on-screen text drafts, and track post performance. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and where that data lives.

Summary: The App has no backend server and no database. Everything you enter stays inside your own web browser, on your own device. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any third party.

2. Data the App Stores

The App stores the following on your device only:

  • Video files you choose to attach, held in your browser's IndexedDB storage
  • Product information you type in — names, prices, promotional text, affiliate links, descriptions and tags
  • Caption and on-screen text drafts generated locally by the App from the information you entered
  • Performance data from analytics CSV files you export from TikTok and import into the App yourself
  • Planning data — your weekly schedule, checklist state and language preference

This data is written to your browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). It is not uploaded, synced, backed up or transmitted anywhere. We have no ability to access it.

3. No Backend Server

The App is a static web page. It performs no network requests to any server we control, because no such server exists. The only external resource the App loads is a public stylesheet library (Tailwind CSS) served from a content delivery network; this is a styling file only and carries none of your data.

4. TikTok API Integration

Status: not currently implemented. The App does not connect to TikTok, does not access your TikTok account, and does not post on your behalf. At present, publishing is done manually by the account holder through TikTok's own web interface.

We have applied for access to TikTok's Content Posting API. If and when that integration is enabled, this section will govern it:

  • Permissions requested: permission to publish video content to the authorising user's own TikTok account, and to upload the video files required to do so.
  • Scope of use: access will be used solely to publish content that the account holder has prepared in the App, to that same account holder's own TikTok account. It will never be used to access, post to, or read data from any other account.
  • Token handling: completing TikTok's OAuth flow for a web application requires a confidential server component to exchange the authorisation code for an access token. If publishing is enabled, that component will be added, its provider will be named in this policy before the feature goes live, and it will store only the access and refresh tokens needed to publish. It will not store video files, captions, or any other content.
  • Revoking access: you can withdraw the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings, under the section listing connected apps. Doing so immediately ends the App's ability to publish.

This policy will be updated, and the "Last Updated" date changed, before any TikTok integration becomes active.

5. No Tracking, Analytics or Advertising

The App contains no analytics tools, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking pixels. No usage data, device identifier or personal information is collected, profiled, sold or shared with anyone. There are no third-party processors, because there is no data flow to process.

6. Retention and Deletion

Data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete everything at any time:

  • In the App, open the Performance tab and use Clear All Data under "Data & backup". This is a two-step confirmation and permanently erases all products, clips, video files, scripts, plans and performance records.
  • Reset performance log only, in the same panel, clears logged results while keeping your products and scripts.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the App from your home screen and clearing site data, also removes everything.

Because the data never leaves your device, deletion on your device is complete deletion. We hold no copy.

7. Age Restriction

The App is not directed to children. It is intended for use by the account holder, who is an adult operating a TikTok Shop affiliate account in accordance with TikTok's own eligibility requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. The "Last Updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. Material changes — in particular, enabling any TikTok account access — will be reflected here before they take effect.
